Stock Clustering

S&P 500 stocks grouped by how their prices have behaved: returns over five windows, volatility, moving-average ratios, beta vs SPY and one-year max drawdown. K-means++ and DBSCAN on the standardized features, projected to two dimensions with PCA.

Not investment advice. This is a study of how stocks group by recent price behaviour. Clusters describe the past year; they do not forecast returns, and the backtest above shows the direction classifier does not beat a naive baseline.

Data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, S&P 500 constituent list and GICS sectors from the datasets/s-and-p-500-companies repository. Source code and method: github.com/darshpandya02/stock-clustering.
